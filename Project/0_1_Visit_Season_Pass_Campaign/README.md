## 0/1 Visit Season Pass Engagement Campaign

### Business Problem

As the season approached its conclusion, analysis identified a large population of season pass holders who had redeemed their pass either zero times or only once throughout the operating season. These customers represented a significant retention and renewal risk because they had not fully utilized the value of their season pass purchase.

### Objective

Increase park visitation among low-engagement season pass holders while improving customer value realization prior to renewal season.

### Audience Strategy

Customers were segmented into:

- 0 Visit Passholders
- 1 Visit Passholders

Each group was further divided based on marketing consent status.

#### Opted-In Customers

Received targeted email offers including:

- 2 Free Fast Lane Passes
- OR
- 2 Single Meal Deals

#### Opted-Out Customers

Received paid social media advertisements designed to encourage park visitation before the end of the season.

### My Role

- Developed Snowflake datasets and dbt models to identify eligible customers.
- Created audience segmentation logic based on visitation behavior and marketing consent preferences.
- Partnered with Marketing teams to prepare campaign-ready audiences for activation.
- Built reporting datasets to track campaign performance and customer engagement throughout the campaign lifecycle.
- Conducted ongoing analysis to monitor visitation trends and measure campaign effectiveness.

### Technical Solution

- Developed dbt models in Snowflake to identify eligible season pass holders.
- Created reusable business logic for low-engagement customer segmentation.
- Automated audience generation processes to support campaign execution.
- Built reporting-ready datasets for ongoing campaign monitoring and performance measurement.
- Leveraged Snowflake dynamic tables to support automated reporting workflows.

### Mid-Campaign Results

- Target Audience: ~201,000 Season Pass Holders
- Returning Visitors: ~40,000 Pass Holders
- Return Rate: ~19.9%

Approximately 40,000 targeted pass holders subsequently visited a Six Flags park during the campaign period.

This campaign provided Marketing stakeholders with actionable audiences designed to re-engage low-utilization season pass holders, improve customer value realization, and support future renewal initiatives. Final campaign results and lift analysis are being evaluated to determine overall impact.
